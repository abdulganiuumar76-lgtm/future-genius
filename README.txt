FUTURE GENIUS — GLOBAL LEARNING EDITION
Creator credit: Abdulganiu Umar

This edition includes:
- A broad starter catalogue across 13 academic departments.
- Search and department filters.
- Country picker covering a broad international list.
- 40 language choices, including English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Mandarin, Hindi and more.
- Education-level and curriculum preference selectors.
- Preferences stored locally in the browser.
- Structured introductory lesson pages for every catalogue subject.
- Curated starter notes, worked examples and practice questions for Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Chemistry, English and Computer Science.
- PWA manifest and basic service-worker caching for HTTPS/localhost hosting.

Limitations:
This is still a prototype, not a published app or production Android APK. The catalogue is broad but not literally every subject in the world. Most subjects currently have a structured introduction rather than a complete textbook or full course. Only selected core subjects have curated notes. Country/curriculum choices are preferences and are not yet automatically matched to official syllabuses. The language selector saves the choice but does not translate all lesson content. AI, sign-in/cloud sync, real payment processing, subscription verification and publishing still need production integrations. Service-worker offline caching requires HTTPS or localhost; opening index.html directly from a file manager may not enable it.

Open:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open Future_Genius/index.html in a browser.
3. Open Global Subject Library / Lesson Library.
4. Search for a subject and tap its card to open the lesson.
5. Use country and language selectors in the top bar.
